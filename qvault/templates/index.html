{# --- What actually happens when a decision is made --------------------------------------- #}
How an action gets authorised
01
Propose
The action is frozen into a canonical payload and hashed. The signer set and threshold are snapshotted, so later membership changes cannot alter what was agreed.
02
Sign, M of N
Each approver unlocks their private key with their password and signs the payload hash. A tally only counts signatures that verify right now.
03
Chain
Every event appends to a SHA-256 hash-chain, each entry committing to the previous entry's hash. Editing history breaks the chain at the edit.
04
Anchor
The head is signed by the SYSTEM post-quantum key. A consistent rewrite moves the head, and a fresh anchor cannot be forged without that key.
