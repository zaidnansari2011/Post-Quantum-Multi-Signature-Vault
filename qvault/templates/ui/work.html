The whole row is one link to the decision; there is no Approve here, because a signature is a deliberate act on the decision page. mode 'mine' (yours to sign: the note says who has approved) or 'others' (due soon, waiting on others: the note says Waiting on N, then who can complete it). total the list's full length; rows is already cut to what is shown. empty the one line a list says when it has nothing (it never disappears). more {label, href}: the footer link to the matching Approvals tab. near_empty the line under the last row when the list ran out early ("Nothing else is due in the next 48 hours"). due(moment, zone=True) A due time: absolute ("Tue 6 Oct, 17:00 UTC"), the relative caption under it, the warning tone and a clock inside 24 hours.
