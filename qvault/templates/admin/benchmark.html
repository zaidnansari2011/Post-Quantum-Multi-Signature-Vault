Every figure is measured through the same CryptoRegistry the application signs and verifies through, so it describes what Q-Vault actually costs. Timings use a monotonic high-resolution clock after a warm-up phase, with the cyclic garbage collector disabled inside the measured loop, and every sample is checked for correctness — a signature that did not verify, or a shared secret that did not agree, aborts the run rather than being reported as a fast result. Medians are shown; the mean is skewed by scheduler noise.
