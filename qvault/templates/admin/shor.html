    What is real and what is scaled

    • The RSA key is 9 bits because a laptop cannot simulate the machine RSA-2048 needs: about {{ '{:,}'.format(shor_2048.logical_qubits) }} logical qubits. The algorithm and its maths are the real ones, and its cost grows polynomially with the key size.
    • The quantum register is simulated exactly: the full state vector is built and the quantum Fourier transform applied to it. The modular exponentiation inside it (the oracle) is computed classically.
    • The forged signature is checked by the same verifier a genuine signature goes through.
    • For ML-DSA the page shows the best known attacks, as published. There is no known quantum attack that breaks it; that is the state of public knowledge, not a proof.
