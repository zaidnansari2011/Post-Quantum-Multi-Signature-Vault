Forgot your password?

We can't reset your password. It also unlocks your signing key, and we never hold a copy of that key we can open, so a reset would lose your signing key. That's why there's no reset link, here or by email. These are the ways back.

What you can do now

  • Use your Recovery Kit

    Type the code from your kit to choose a new password and keep your signing key. It takes 72 hours, and you, your workspace's admins and your fellow approvers are warned meanwhile and can cancel it.

    {{ button('Use your Recovery Kit', 'primary', href=url_for('recover.kit'), block=True) }}

  • Approve from your paired phone

    A phone you paired holds its own signing key, which this password doesn't unlock. It stays signed in for {{ device_days }} days from when you paired it, and you can approve from it until then. Pairing a phone again needs the password.

  • Start again with a new account

    A workspace owner or admin can invite another email address of yours. Each vault's owner can then add the new account and remove this one. Your past signatures stay valid. Two things can't move to the new account yet: a vault you own, and a workspace nobody else manages.

Coming, not built yet

  • A new web password from your phone

    In the app, choose "Set a new web password". It takes 24 hours and gives you a new web signing key.

  • Key replacement by your approvers

    A workspace admin starts a key replacement and your vault's approvers approve a new signing key for you, as a decision, with time for any of them to cancel it.

Neither is built yet. Until they are, the three ways above are the only ones.
