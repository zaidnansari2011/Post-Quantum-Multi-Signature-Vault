Forgot your password?

We can't reset your password. It also unlocks your signing key, which we store only encrypted under it, so a reset would lose your signing key. That's why there's no reset link, here or by email.

What you can do now

  • Approve from your paired phone

    A phone you paired holds its own signing key, which this password doesn't unlock. It stays signed in for {{ device_days }} days from when you paired it, and you can approve from it until then. Pairing a phone again needs the password.

  • Start again with a new account

    A workspace owner or admin can invite another email address of yours. Each vault's owner can then add the new account and remove this one. Your past signatures stay valid. Two things can't move to the new account yet: a vault you own, and a workspace nobody else manages.

Coming, not built yet

  • Key replacement

    Your vault's approvers approve a new signing key for you, as a decision, with time for any of them to cancel it.

  • Recovery Kit

    A code you save when you sign up, which unlocks a second copy of your signing key.

Neither is built yet. Until they are, the two routes above are the only ones.
